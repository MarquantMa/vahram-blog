### New York, New York — October 15, 2026

Typography on a screen is not simply the rendering of glyphs; it is the physical architecture of thought. 

When you look closely at **Apple Newsroom**, what catches your attention is not loud decorations or aggressive popups. What strikes you is the disciplined harmony of the layout:

- **The Eyebrow**: A subtle uppercase anchor setting the stage with wide tracking (`0.08em`).
- **The Display Headline**: Bold, commanding yet never overwhelming, tightly tracked to feel sculptured.
- **The Deck**: A gentle bridge that summarizes core ideas before the reader dives into the prose.
- **The Dateline**: Rooted in journalistic integrity, linking time and physical origin.

```css
/* Apple Newsroom typographic harmony */
.eyebrow {
  font-size: 0.75rem;
  font-weight: 600;
  text-transform: uppercase;
  letter-spacing: 0.08em;
  color: #86868b;
}

.headline {
  font-weight: 700;
  letter-spacing: -0.025em;
  line-height: 1.15;
}
```

When visual noise falls silent, comprehension takes center stage.

### 纽约州纽约市 — 2026年10月15日

屏幕上的排版设计绝不仅仅是字形的机械渲染；它是思想在数字空间中的建筑骨架。

当你细致审视 **Apple Newsroom** 时，最打动人心的绝不是喧宾夺主的装饰或突兀的弹窗，而是整个版面中所蕴含的极其自律的比例美感：

- **眉标 (The Eyebrow)**：小号大写字母，以舒展的字距（`0.08em`）定下整篇报道的主题基调。
- **主标题 (The Display Headline)**：厚重有力、张弛有度，略带收敛的字距呈现出如雕塑般洗练的视觉密度。
- **副标题 (The Deck)**：温润舒缓的过渡桥梁，在读者进入正文之前以优雅的笔调凝练核心。
- **新闻发生地线 (The Dateline)**：承袭自经典新闻学传统，清晰标注时间与地理坐标。

```css
/* Apple Newsroom 经典排版比例 */
.eyebrow {
  font-size: 0.75rem;
  font-weight: 600;
  text-transform: uppercase;
  letter-spacing: 0.08em;
  color: #86868b;
}

.headline {
  font-weight: 700;
  letter-spacing: -0.025em;
  line-height: 1.15;
}
```

当视觉上的所有杂音归于寂静，思考与理解便自然走向舞台的中央。
