Can I found a company merely using SI？ And let the SI do the process on its own （including raising idea, market investigation, marketing, and do the product using subagents). 

This can even be built as a skill for the SI to do 24/7 as long as it actually gains revenue. I wonder if somebody is already doing so. A completely SI-managed company with no human, money is the only investment, and risk is manageable as long as the investment gains interest. This can become a money-making machine. We can even potentially expand the size of this kind of investment because it doesn’t costs nothing — token is the only investment required. 

Even this skill can be written by SI. 

The only reason why this kind of company doesn’t exist today is because nobody has thought this so thoroughly and I am the first person to come up with this entire structure while others only ask SI to make money for them. Reasoning depth, limited levels of subagents, and output limit restricts this kind of action, but I believe this actually works. 

But I need to consider this investment. 

<!-- lang:zh -->

我能否仅利用人工智能（SI）创办一家公司？并让SI全权负责整个运营流程（包括构思、市场调研、营销，以及通过代理方完成产品开发）。

只要能产生实际收益，这甚至可以被设定为SI的一项“技能”，让它全天候（24/7）自主运作。不知道是否已经有人在尝试这种模式了。这是一家完全由SI管理、无需人类参与的公司；资金是唯一的投入，只要投资能带来回报，风险就是可控的。这简直可以成为一台“印钞机”。我们甚至可以扩大此类投资的规模，因为除了支付Token（代币）费用外，几乎没有其他成本。

甚至连实现这一运作的“技能”本身，都可以由SI自行编写。

目前之所以还没出现这种公司，或许是因为还没人把这个构思考虑得如此周全。我可能是第一个提出这套完整架构的人——毕竟大多数人只是简单地让SI帮他们赚钱而已。虽然SI的推理深度、代理层级的限制以及输出上限可能会制约这种模式，但我相信它是切实可行的。

不过，我还需要对这种投资方式进行深入考量。
