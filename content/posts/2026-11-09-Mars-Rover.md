I think today is a real great day for my Mars Rover project. 

Amongst all the difficulties I conquered to get to this point, today is with the highest efficiency. 

Even though there’s still a long way to go to really make it work, I think the day to that is compressed by 1/10 to the expected time. 

Using google antigravity agent with Argon 4 high model, I debugged the project through CLI.  It even helped me compose the network to log into SDGJ with my account. This helps me get to track the problems a lot more efficiently. Another great thing to mention is by using a power bank and the two 3.7V rechargeable batteries, the servo, step motor, camera, as well as the edge VLM worked very well. 

Although the recognition accuracy is still limited, I am expecting to train another model using the real handbags to make it work next time. 

I think the biggest takeaway I got today is really you don’t need to be ready, you just need to begin. 

Think about the first time I heard about this from Mark Zuckerberg’s commencement at Harvard 2005, I was inspired. This has been the motto I said to myself every single morning I wake up last year. 

It is this motto that led me to found my InkYard, STEMars, and RawSTEAM. In my hardest time, this motto keeps me going in spite of the difficulties and trauma. 

I have been in a mindset of feeling that I will lose this time that could be used to write my personal statement, improve my CV, and writing PIQ for MET. 

I fear that I will spend the whole night on my project and finish nothing. 

I was afraid that someone will see me restarting my project, fail, and joke on me being a failure. 

I was afraid doing the project will make people pick up their stereotype on me and think I’m back to the state of ignorance and hating people. 

Today, it is proved again. The moment when I opened Arduino App Lab, I figured how this can work after exactly four months of stagnation. In the beginning, I was figuring what each file and hardware does. It was a real hard friction, but as I moved on, the dopamine of achievement and feeling of approaching success makes me doing it. When it starts off, the feeling of not knowing what each component does really made me feel this robot belonged to someone else. But having worked on it for thirty minutes, that feeling vanished like the dust accumulated on the bot. 

Sometimes, start-up friction, other people’s sayings, and your fear of losing really keeps you from starting, but once you start, you are unstoppable. Some people are planning their entire life, but never achieving something. Some people just starts whatever, and they lose, but they learn, and achieve. 

I just wanna say: I still love what I do.

我认为今天对于我的火星探测车项目来说，真的是很棒的一天。

在克服重重困难走到今天这一步的过程中，今天的效率是最高的。

虽然要让它真正完美运转还有很长的路要走，但我认为距离目标达成的时间比预期缩短了十分之一。

使用 Google Antigravity Agent 和 Argon 4 High 模型，我通过 CLI（命令行）调试了整个项目。它甚至帮我配置了网络，用我的账号登录了 SDGJ。这让我能够更高效地排查问题。另一个值得一提的是，通过使用一个充电宝和两节 3.7V 可充电电池，舵机、步进电机、摄像头以及边缘端 VLM 都运行得非常顺利。

虽然识别精度目前仍然有限，但我期待下次用真实的手袋来训练另一个模型，让它能正常工作。

我觉得今天我最大的收获其实是：你不需要先做好万全准备，你只需要开始。

回想起我第一次在马克·扎克伯格的哈佛毕业典礼演讲中听到这句话时，深受启发。去年，这句话成了我每天早晨醒来都会对自己默念的座右铭。

正是这句座右铭激励我创立了 InkYard、STEMars 和 RawSTEAM。在我最艰难的时刻，也是这句座右铭支撑着我战胜种种困难与创伤。

此前我一直陷入一种焦虑心态，总觉得自己是在浪费本该用来写个人陈述（PS）、修改简历以及为 MET 准备 PIQ 的时间。

我害怕自己熬了一整夜做项目，最后却一无所获。

我害怕别人看到我重新启动项目后再次失败，然后嘲笑我是个失败者。

我也害怕做这个项目会让人加深对我的刻板印象，觉得我又回到了无知和仇视他人的状态。

今天，事实再次证明了这一点。当我打开 Arduino App Lab 的那一刻，在停滞了整整四个月之后，我终于摸索出它是如何运作的了。刚开始时，我还在费力弄清楚每个文件和硬件的作用，启动阻力非常大；但随着我一步步推进，那种成就感的多巴胺以及离成功越来越近的感觉推着我不断向前。刚开始的时候，因为不清楚每个组件是干什么的，真让我觉得这台机器人像是别人的。但沉浸在里面做了三十分钟后，那种陌生感就如同落在车身上的灰尘一样烟消云散了。

很多时候，启动阻力、别人的风言风语，以及对失败的恐惧，都会阻止你迈出第一步。但一旦你开始了，你就势不可挡。有些人规划了一辈子，却终究一事无成；有些人哪怕什么都不懂就直接动手，他们会遭遇失败，但他们能从中学到东西，并最终做成事情。

我只想说：我依然深爱我所做的一切。
