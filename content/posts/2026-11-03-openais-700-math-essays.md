Two weeks ago, OpenAI published 700 math essays that totally bombs the mathematicians.

A very interesting reflection on this is the one I listened to today. Many people say that mathematics will keep existing because only math resolved by humans is valid.

I think this is exactly the same thing that happens in industrial revolutions in history. When book copiers are replaced by printers, food producers are replaced by machines, human computers are replaced by digital computers, people said the same thing.

Although I’m not the directly shattered ones, I think this will be the same for most industries in the world.

If this really happens, where will human value lie?

When the human are replaced by computers, the value lies in the way to use computers efficiently to sort and organize data.

Every time a revolution happens in an industry, the value goes to a new field that uses this tool instead of keeping up in the same position and insist doing the same thing. This has happened. Someone today may still copy the books, and the food is still produced by humans. But this value really depends on whether people would still keep their old ideology and emphasis.

The field that used to be the most time consuming may be accelerated by SI, but the rest will become more important. Whether you can ask the right question, whether the propagation of this knowledge and communication will be effective enough.

I believe the same will happen in at least 10 fields that are used to be considered as prestige. Engineers will not be able to escape, and that’s why I will not be the ones to do the parts that will be or already capable by SI.

Someone may keep typing in the source code on their own. The value of this remains because some hack code may not be capable by SI. However, most repetitive typing is already very replaceable.

When I fill in the personal information and academic records in UC college portal, the very only thing I do is transcribing the prompt to Antigravity, telling it where to find my official transcript, and double-check after it finishes using Chromium.

Therefore, the value that used to lie in the most time consuming, which is coding and debugging, now becomes coming up with the right idea that provides values more than the cost and writing the right documentation for propagation. These may not be viable for SI for a very extended period. SI’s perception of human nature and real world problems are still very limited, and even if they come up with the right idea and make the product, they might not be ideal to people, so the right propaganda is required.

Keeping coming up with practical ideas is very difficult even for humans, so this will be the next privileged work. High brain consuming, very few people are capable of.

As many may not know till now, SI can already do easy PCB design and schematics, which might dramatically draw down the cost of designing a new chip architecture.

Use of SI is very limitedly explored until today, and I believe there will be a lot more explored.

两周前，OpenAI 发布了 700 篇数学论文，令数学界大为震惊。

今天我听到了一种很有意思的观点：许多人认为数学将永远存在，因为只有人类推导出的数学才具有效力。

我认为，这与历史上工业革命期间发生的情况如出一辙。当抄书员被印刷机取代、粮食生产者被机器取代、从事计算工作的人（“人肉计算机”）被数字计算机取代时，人们当时也持有同样的论调。

虽然我本人并未直接受到这种冲击，但我认为，全球大多数行业都将面临同样的局面。

如果这种情况真的发生，人类的价值又将何在？

当人类被计算机取代时，人类的价值便在于：如何高效地利用计算机来整理和组织数据。

每当某个行业发生革命，价值重心就会转移到能够运用新工具的领域，而非固守在原有的岗位上重复旧有的工作。这种转变在历史上早有先例。即便在今天，或许仍有人在从事抄书工作，人类也依然参与着粮食生产；但这种“价值”究竟如何，很大程度上取决于人们是否仍执着于旧有的观念与侧重点。

那些曾经最耗时的环节，可能会在智能系统（SI）的加持下大幅提速，而其他环节则会变得愈发重要——例如，你是否能提出正确的问题？知识的传播与沟通是否足够高效？

我相信，至少有 10 个曾经被视为“高端”或“体面”的领域，都将经历同样的变革。工程师群体也无法置身事外；正因如此，我绝不会去从事那些“智能系统已经能够完成”或“未来终将被其取代”的工作。

或许仍有人坚持亲手敲入每一行源代码——这种做法或许仍有其价值，因为某些“非标准”或“奇巧”的代码逻辑，智能系统未必能处理。然而，绝大多数重复性的编码工作，早已变得极易被取代。

以我在加州大学（UC）申请系统中填写个人信息和学术记录为例：我所做的仅仅是将申请表单中的要求“转述”给智能系统（Antigravity），告知它去哪里获取我的官方成绩单，并在它通过浏览器（Chromium）完成操作后，进行简单的核对即可。因此，过去那些最耗时的工作——即编码与调试——其价值已发生转移。如今，核心价值在于：提出能够创造出远超其自身成本之价值的构想，并撰写出恰当的文档以进行推广。在相当长的一段时期内，这种模式对于“合成智能”（SI）而言或许尚不可行。SI 对人性及现实世界问题的理解依然非常有限；即便它们能提出正确的构想并将其转化为产品，这些产品也未必能真正契合人们的需求，因此，有效的推广至关重要。

持续提出切实可行的构想，即便对人类而言也绝非易事；因此，这将成为下一阶段极具门槛的工作——它高度依赖脑力，且仅有极少数人能够胜任。

或许许多人至今尚不了解，SI 实际上已经能够完成简单的 PCB 设计与原理图绘制，这有望大幅降低设计新芯片架构的成本。

迄今为止，人们对 SI 的应用探索还非常有限；我相信，未来仍有巨大的探索空间等待挖掘。
