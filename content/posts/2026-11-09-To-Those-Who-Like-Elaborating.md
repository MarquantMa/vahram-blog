To those who love elaborating:

There are people out there who actually swap the housing on their phone just to turn an iPhone 12 into an "iPhone 17 Pro." 

And after pulling off such an embarrassing stunt, they go around calling their iPhone 12 an “iPhone 17 Pro” to everyone they meet.

Inevitably, someone asks: “Wait, how come your iPhone 17 Pro doesn't have a Dynamic Island?”

Caught red-handed, she brushes it off: “Oh, I just swapped the shell. It looks great, what’s the big deal?”

Yet every single time she mentions her phone, she still insists on calling it a “17 Pro.” She only ever tells the truth when someone corners her with questions she can't dodge. 

And the audacity doesn't stop there. Whenever she catches someone using an older or cheaper phone, she sneers: “Ugh, I’d throw that straight into the trash.”

To the naive and easily fooled, she looks like some trendy, high-end tastemaker. But to anyone with working eyes, she’s just a clown playing dress-up—acting as if she has some exclusive backdoor connection with John Ternus himself to hook her up with special privileges.

In the end, she’s the only one suffering under her own charade:
- She has to open a friend’s file on a Windows PC and scrambles to claim it’s just a “technical glitch.”
- She has to obsessively guard its appearance every waking second, terrified someone will spot the fake seams and the telltale notch.
- She has to constantly make excuses and insist it “works just as well,” desperately trying to convince people she’s carrying the latest flagship.

If living a lie takes that much work, you aren't flexing—you're just exhausting yourself.

<!-- lang:zh -->

致那些爱装腔作势的人：

有些人就是能干出这种事：专门换了个改装后盖，把一台 iPhone 12 硬生生改造成所谓的“iPhone 17 Pro”。

做完这种令人脚趾扣地的尴尬勾当后，她还逢人就自豪地宣称自己手里的是“iPhone 17 Pro”。

终于有人忍不住问了：“等等，你这 iPhone 17 Pro 怎么连灵动岛都没有？”

被当场拆穿的她轻描淡写地狡辩道：“哦，其实我就改了个壳，挺好用的啊，有什么问题吗？”

可滑稽的是，每次只要提起自己的手机，她嘴里依旧一口一个“17 Pro”。只有被别人当面揪出破绽、逼问到脸上时，她才会不情不愿地吐露半句实话。

更讽刺的是，当看到别人用着旧款或者便宜的机型时，她反倒居高临下地鄙夷道：“呵，换成是我，早扔垃圾桶里了。”

在那些容易被蒙骗的人眼里，她是个走在时尚前沿、光鲜亮丽的“高端人士”。但在任何眼睛没瞎的人眼里，她不过是个跳梁小丑——装模作样，演得好像自己跟苹果硬件副总裁约翰·特纳斯（John Ternus）有什么特殊私交，能享受到什么顶级特权一样。

可到头来，真正受罪的只有她自己：
- 借朋友的 Windows 电脑传个文件查看一下，露馅了还得硬说是“技术故障”；
- 平时拿在手里小心翼翼、如履薄冰，生怕别人多看两眼识破那套山寨外壳和那块尴尬的刘海屏；
- 整天还要费尽唇舌去辩解“用起来性能完全一样”，拼命向周围人自证自己用的是最前沿的旗舰。

如果维持虚荣需要活得这么狼狈，那真不是在显摆，纯粹是在自我折磨。
